      Questions

      I've never used AI. Can I do this?

      Yes. If you can copy and paste, you can use it. You answer a few questions, copy one piece of text and paste it into an AI helper such as ChatGPT. Then you paste in job adverts and it tells you which are worth your time. You can't break anything, and it never sends or applies for anything.

      Is ApplySteward free?

      Yes. Setting up your rules, the job site suggestions, sorting jobs, writing messages and the weekly review are all free for personal use. A low, one-off Pro pack with CV tailoring, interview practice and career-specific packs is coming. There is no subscription.

      Does it apply for jobs for me?

      No. It screens, tracks and drafts. You decide what to send and you press send every time. That protects your reputation and your accounts on job sites.

      Which AI do I need?

      Any one of Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or Microsoft Copilot, and the free versions are enough to start. Having your inbox checked for you each morning currently needs a paid Claude plan.

      Is my data safe?

      What you type on this site stays on your own device. There is no account to create here, and we never see your answers, your CV or your emails. They go only where you paste them: into your own AI helper. On a shared computer, press Clear my answers before you leave.

      Which job boards should I use?

      The setup questions suggest the main job sites for your country and specialist ones for your field, such as NHS Jobs, eFinancialCareers or Tes. You ask those sites to email you new jobs each day, and your AI helper does the sorting.
