    Your setup

    So the next steps match the tools you already use. Where a small change would help, we'll say why. Every suggestion is optional.

    Where do your job alerts arrive?
    Consider a Gmail address just for job alerts Daily scans read alerts through Claude's Gmail connection. You can keep your main inbox and forward job alerts to a free Gmail address. It takes about five minutes and keeps job mail out of your personal inbox too.
    Move alerts off your work address If you're still on notice or garden leave, your employer may be able to see that inbox. A separate Gmail address keeps your search private.
    Where do you find jobs now? Tick all that apply.
    Turn your searches into email alerts Your aide can only screen what lands in your inbox. Save each search on the boards you use as a daily email alert, and follow your target companies' career pages where they offer alerts.
    What format is your main CV in?
    Keep an editable master copy Tailoring works by editing text. A Word or Google Docs version keeps your layout intact. Most PDFs can be opened in Word to get one.
    Which AI do you use?
    You can start today, and automate later The free skills work by pasting alerts into a chat. Automatic daily scans need a paid Claude plan, which you can add when you're ready.
    Built for Claude first Your rules file is plain text and works anywhere. Paste it into ChatGPT with your alerts for a manual check. The skills and daily scans are Claude-only for now.
