Privacy

What happens to your information, in plain words.

The short version. ApplySteward has no accounts and no database. What you type here is kept on your own device. It is never sent to us, and nobody else can see it.

What stays on your device

Your answers to the setup questions and the list of jobs on your desk are saved inside the web browser you are using, on the phone or computer in front of you. That is how the site remembers them when you come back.

  • They are not sent to ApplySteward or to anyone else.
  • We cannot see them, and we could not recover them for you if they were lost.
  • They do not follow you to another device. Press Save a copy if you want a backup.

On a shared or public computer

Because your answers stay in the browser, the next person to use the same computer and browser could open this site and see them. Before you leave a library, work or family computer, press Clear my answers on the setup page and Clear this device on your desk.

What you share with your AI helper

ApplySteward works by having you copy text and paste it into an AI helper such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Copilot. Whatever you paste there, including your rules and any job adverts, is handled by that company under its own privacy terms, not ours. Other people cannot see it, but it is worth knowing where it goes.

  • Only paste what you are comfortable sharing with that company.
  • You do not need to include your address, date of birth or any ID numbers, and we suggest you don't.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, passwords or sign-up.
  • No cookies.
  • No advertising, tracking or visitor statistics.
  • No selling or sharing of information. We hold none to sell.

What the website's host can see

Like every website, this one is delivered by a hosting company. Ours is GitHub. When your browser loads a page, GitHub's computers receive the usual technical details of the visit, such as your internet (IP) address and the time, and GitHub may keep them for security. This never includes anything you type on the site.

Links to other sites

The job sites and AI helpers we link to are run by other companies. Once you follow a link, their own privacy terms apply.

If you email us

If you write to yserrag@yahoo.com, we will have your email address and whatever you choose to tell us, and we use them only to reply. Ask at any time and we will delete the conversation.

If this changes

If ApplySteward ever adds a feature that needs your information to leave your device, this page will say so first, and the feature will ask before it does anything.
